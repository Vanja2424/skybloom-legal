Help & support

A cozy match-3 adventure by Via Studio

Need a hand? Email support@viastudio.org. The quickest way is the Contact button in the game (Settings → About): it adds your player ID, game version and device so we can help faster.

Common questions

How do I keep my progress on a new phone? Progress is saved on your device. Android's backup can carry it over when you set up a new phone from a backup of the old one.

Lives and timers seem wrong after I changed my clock. The game keeps its own time so timers can't be skipped; it sorts itself out once the device has the right time and is online.

How do I turn off notifications? In the game: Settings → Game → Notifications. Or in your phone's settings for Skybloom.

How do I start again from level 1? In the village: Settings → About → Start over. This can't be undone.
