Delete your Skybloom data

Skybloom by Via Studio · Last updated: 14 November 2026

In short: Skybloom has no accounts, and your game progress is saved only on your own device. The only things we may hold are feedback you sent from the game and emails you sent to support. You can ask us to delete them at any time.

How to ask us to delete your data

  1. In Skybloom, open Settings → About and note your player ID (it looks like SB-ABCD-EFGH).
  2. Email support@viastudio.org with the subject "Delete my Skybloom data" and your player ID. If you've emailed us before, send it from the same address so we can find those emails too.
  3. We delete your data within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What we delete

  • Every feedback message sent from the game with your player ID (the message, star rating, game version, device model, system version and language).
  • Your emails to support and our replies.

What we keep, and for how long

Only the email in which you asked for deletion, and our reply, as a record that we did it. Like any support email, it is deleted within 12 months.

Data on your device

Your progress, settings, year of birth, ads answer and purchase order numbers never leave your device, so we can't delete them for you, but you can: use Settings → About → Start over in the game, or uninstall Skybloom (or clear its data in your device's settings). Things you bought to keep (like "Remove ads") come back from Google Play if you reinstall.

Ad partners

Ads are shown through Unity LevelPlay and its ad networks, which handle their data under their own policies. You can reset or delete your device's advertising ID in your device's settings, and ask Unity to delete data through Unity's Privacy Policy. More in our Privacy Policy.
