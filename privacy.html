Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

In short: Skybloom has no accounts. It doesn't ask for your name, email, contacts, location or photos, and your progress is saved on your own device. The game is free thanks to ads. It asks your year of birth once: players who may be under 16 only ever get ads that aren't personalised and are marked as meant for a younger player. Players 16 and over are asked whether our ad partners may use the device's advertising ID, and can change that answer any time in Settings. Purchases are handled by Google Play; we never see your card.

Who we are

Skybloom is made by Via Studio ("we", "us"). If you have any question about this policy or your data, write to us at support@viastudio.org.

Your device's operating system may back up the game's storage (for example to your Google account) under your own device settings. That backup is handled by your device's provider under their privacy policy.

What the game sends over the internet

The time. The game asks a Google server (google.com) for the current time, so that timers for lives and daily rewards can't be fooled by changing the device clock. The request carries no information about you or your game. As with any internet request, Google's server sees your IP address; Google's handling of it is covered by Google's Privacy Policy.

Ads. Skybloom shows ads through Unity LevelPlay, run by Unity Technologies, which lets several ad networks (Unity Ads among them) offer an ad for each slot. You choose to watch some ads for a reward (an extra life, extra moves or a booster); others play between levels unless you've bought "Remove ads". To show and count ads, and to stop fraud, LevelPlay and the ad networks receive your IP address, your device type, operating system and language, and how you interact with the ads.

The first time you start the game, it asks the year you were born. If you may be under 16, the game doesn't ask about personalised ads: your ads are never personalised, the ad networks are told the player may be a child (so they don't use the advertising ID and only show ads suitable for children), and only ad networks Google has approved for younger players are used. If you're 16 or over, the game then asks whether our ad partners may also use your device's advertising ID to pick ads that suit you. Nothing is sent to any ad network before you answer. If you say yes, the advertising ID can be used for personalised ads. If you say no, you still see the same number of ads, but they aren't picked using what is known about you, and for players in California this also means your information isn't sold or shared. You can change your answer any time in Settings → About → Privacy & terms → Ads, and you can reset or delete the advertising ID in your device's settings. In Europe and the UK we rely on your consent for personalised ads, and on our legitimate interest in keeping the game free for the ads themselves. Unity's handling of this data is covered by Unity's Privacy Policy.

Purchases. If you buy something in the game, Google Play takes the payment under Google's Privacy Policy. The game only receives what you bought and an order number, which it keeps on your device so the same order is never paid out twice and so things kept for good (like "Remove ads") come back if you reinstall. We never see your card or payment details.

Feedback. If you use Tell us what you think, the game sends what you write, your star rating, your player ID, the game version, your device model, its system version and the game's language to our database at Google Firebase, stored in the EU, where only we can read it. Nothing is sent until you press Send. We use it to improve the game and, if we decide to thank you, to make a gift code that only works for your player ID. Please don't write your name, email or address in it. We delete feedback within 24 months, or sooner if you send us your player ID and ask. Google's handling of it is covered by Firebase's privacy information.

The game has no analytics or social services. If that changes in a future version, we'll update this policy first and describe exactly what is shared, with whom, and why.

Notifications

If you allow them, the game reminds you when your lives are full or a gift is waiting. These reminders are scheduled on your device by the game itself: no server sends them and nothing is shared. You can turn them off in the game's Settings or in your device's settings at any time.

Emails to support

If you write to us, we'll see your email address and whatever you include (the game pre-fills your player ID, game version and device model to help us help you). We use it only to answer you, and we delete support emails within 12 months.

Children

Skybloom is meant for players aged 13 and over. Because the rules for younger players differ from country to country (in much of Europe, children under 16 can't agree to personalised ads by themselves), the game asks the year of birth once, without suggesting an answer, and treats everyone who may be under 16 as a younger player: no personalised ads, ads marked as meant for a child, and no question about the advertising ID. The year of birth stays on your device. If you believe a younger player gave the wrong year, delete and reinstall the game (or clear its data in your device's settings) to answer again, or write to us if you'd like us to help.

Your rights

Because your game data stays on your device, you're always in control: deleting the game (or using "Start over" in Settings) erases it. You can withdraw your consent to personalised ads at any time in Settings, without affecting anything before. For data held by Unity or the ad networks, you can also contact them directly under their privacy policies. If you've emailed us or sent feedback and want it deleted, or want to know what we hold, write to us and we'll handle it within 30 days (how to ask). Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California) you have rights to access, correct and delete your personal data, and to complain to your data protection authority.

Security

Saved progress is encrypted and signed on your device, and every connection the game makes uses HTTPS.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the game.
