Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

In short: Skybloom has no accounts. It doesn't ask for your name, email, contacts, location or photos, and it doesn't sell or share anything about you. Your progress is saved on your own device.

Who we are

Skybloom is made by Via Studio ("we", "us"). If you have any question about this policy or your data, write to us at support@viastudio.com.

What the game keeps, and where

WhatWhyWhere
Your game progress: levels, stars, coins, lives, boosters, village, settingsSo you can carry on where you left offOnly on your device, in the game's private storage (encrypted)
A random player ID (like SB-ABCD-EFGH)So you can quote it if you ask us for help. It is made up at random and isn't linked to you, your device or any account.Only on your device; we see it only if you send it to us

Your device's operating system may back up the game's storage (for example to your Google account) under your own device settings. That backup is handled by your device's provider under their privacy policy.

What the game sends over the internet

The game only asks a Google server (google.com) for the current time, so that timers for lives and daily rewards can't be fooled by changing the device clock. The request carries no information about you or your game. As with any internet request, Google's server sees your IP address; Google's handling of it is covered by Google's Privacy Policy.

The game has no advertising, analytics, social, or payment services. If that changes in a future version, we'll update this policy first and describe exactly what is shared, with whom, and why.

Notifications

If you allow them, the game reminds you when your lives are full or a gift is waiting. These reminders are scheduled on your device by the game itself: no server sends them and nothing is shared. You can turn them off in the game's Settings or in your device's settings at any time.

Emails to support

If you write to us, we'll see your email address and whatever you include (the game pre-fills your player ID, game version and device model to help us help you). We use it only to answer you, and we delete support emails within 12 months.

Children

Skybloom is suitable for all ages. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, children included: the game works without any.

Your rights

Because your game data stays on your device, you're always in control: deleting the game (or using "Start over" in Settings) erases it. If you've emailed us and want that correspondence deleted, or want to know what we hold, write to us and we'll handle it within 30 days. Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California) you have rights to access, correct and delete your personal data, and to complain to your data protection authority.

Security

Saved progress is encrypted and signed on your device, and every connection the game makes uses HTTPS.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the game.
